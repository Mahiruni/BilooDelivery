# Product delivery roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- Monorepo structure and shared packages
- Premium blue and amber design system
- Customer onboarding, authentication shell, discovery, restaurant detail, cart, order tracking, and profile flows
- Driver and vendor operational shells
- Admin operations dashboard shell
- Initial Supabase schema, indexes, constraints, triggers, and Row Level Security
- CI checks and developer setup documentation

## Phase 2 — Connected customer MVP

- Supabase authentication and profile onboarding
- Address search and saved delivery locations
- Vendor/menu synchronization
- Persistent cart and checkout
- Cash and online payment orchestration
- Order creation, status timeline, and push notifications

## Phase 3 — Vendor operations

- Vendor staff roles and permissions
- Live incoming order queue
- Preparation-time updates and item availability
- Menu, modifiers, inventory, opening hours, and promotions
- Sales and fulfilment analytics

## Phase 4 — Driver fulfilment

- Driver verification and vehicle documents
- Availability and dispatch offers
- Pickup/drop-off workflow
- Background location updates and live customer tracking
- Earnings, payouts, incidents, and proof of delivery

## Phase 5 — Marketplace operations

- Admin role matrix and audit trail
- Vendor/driver approval workflows
- Order intervention and refunds
- Service zones, fees, commissions, and surge controls
- Support tooling, reports, fraud signals, and reconciliation

## Phase 6 — Release and scale

- Android and iOS signing
- Store assets, privacy declarations, and release tracks
- Crash reporting, performance monitoring, and alerting
- Load tests, backup restoration tests, and disaster-recovery runbooks
- Maintenance cadence and product analytics
